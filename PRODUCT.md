# ActorConsole

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Web. The existing React application supports desktop and mobile browsers, light and dark themes, and keyboard access.

## Users and purpose

Apify builders managing an actor portfolio. ActorConsole brings actor synchronization, workspace operations, research, usage analytics, pricing, listing copy, publishing, and MCP access into one workspace.

## Plans and billing

Free includes the operational workflows for up to 50 actors AND 500 summed actor users over the past 30 days per workspace. A person can count across multiple actors. Pro removes both workspace limits; it does not reserve tools or a support tier exclusively for paying customers.

Prices and offered billing intervals are configured by administrators. Prefer live offers over test offers and clearly label test checkouts. Never invent prices, savings, testimonials, or exclusive benefits. Collect and successfully save a billing address before opening checkout. Upgrade only after confirmed payment. Existing subscription controls and payment history must remain accessible.

Present monthly pricing first and show available annual savings. Pricing and workspace billing should make plan differences easy to compare and encourage a clear, informed path to Pro.

## Brand commitments

Preserve ActorConsole's existing theme-aware paper, ink, and restrained orange system for these pricing and billing improvements. No global rebrand is requested.
